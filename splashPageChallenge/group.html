Research

Market Research

Separating external evidence from working hypotheses and unknowns that require local validation.

Supported by external research

  • Menu quality and ease of use matter.
  • QR menus and QR ordering address different needs.
  • Customer convenience and usability should be considered.

Working hypotheses

  • Independent cafes may be easier to approach than large chains.
  • Personalized videos may help initiate conversations with owners.
  • Direct outreach may generate qualified conversations faster than waiting for organic content.

Unknowns requiring local validation

  • Restaurant willingness to pay.
  • Local demand for digital menus.
  • Preferred pricing.
  • QR ordering adoption.
  • Which acquisition channel produces the best paying-customer conversion.

Validation

Customer Acquisition Experiments

Two proposed experiments with editable tracking tables.

Experiment 1: Personalized vs. general TikTok videos

Hypothesis
Personalized videos generate more qualified conversations than general content.
Variables to keep consistent
Posting time, CTA, offer, follow-up timing.
Metrics to compare
DM replies, meetings booked, qualified leads.
Potential confounders
Audience size, editing quality, topic choice.
Decision criteria
Choose the format with more qualified leads per hour of effort.

Experiment 2: Direct outreach vs. video-assisted outreach

Group A Direct outreach only.
Group B Personalized video + direct outreach.

Keep the offer, follow-up timing, and lead qualification rules comparable across both groups.

Sales

Sales Process

A seven-step pipeline with purpose, action, and recorded information.

  1. Identify the business. Purpose: build the prospect list. Action: research segment and use case. Record: name, segment, contact.
  2. Reach the decision-maker. Purpose: start a conversation. Action: visit or message the owner/manager. Record: date, method, outcome.
  3. Discover needs. Purpose: understand priorities. Action: ask about menu and ordering. Record: quotes, pain points.
  4. Demonstrate Mela QR. Purpose: show the product. Action: run a short demo. Record: demo date, reaction.
  5. Offer a pilot. Purpose: reduce risk. Action: propose a small trial. Record: pilot terms, start date.
  6. Follow up. Purpose: keep momentum. Action: schedule next contact. Record: follow-up dates.
  7. Convert and document. Purpose: close the loop. Action: confirm outcome. Record: result, reasons, lessons.

Sample first-contact message

Hello [Name], I'm [Your Name] from Mela QR. We help cafes and restaurants in Addis Ababa offer a digital menu and optional QR ordering. Would you be open to a short 10-minute demo? No commitment — just to see if it fits your place.

Common objections

  • Price. Record the number and compare to perceived value.
  • Connectivity. Ask about wifi and customer data use.
  • Lack of perceived need. Note what triggers interest.
  • Customer preference. Offer to keep printed menus available.
  • Implementation effort. Show how simple setup is.

Document objections during each conversation and use them to improve the sales process.
